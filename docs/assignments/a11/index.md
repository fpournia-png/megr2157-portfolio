### Vertical Frame FEA 
* For this part for the assignment I conducted FEA on the vertical components of the frame in order to analyze the effect forces will have on our frame. I used Solidworks to conduct this analysis and run my simulation. I first isolated one of the vertical columns in CAD and then assigned the correct material of 6063-T4 Aluminum then proceeded to assign two forces to the column. I applied one axial compression force of 2 kN as a result of the top of the frame pushing down on the column. The second force I added to the simulation was the torque of 6 N that the motor will exert on the column, this force was added to the side of the column around the center axis. After assigning my forces I proceeded to add in a fixed fixture for the bottom of the column.

* In order to run a convergence check I ran two simulations with two different types of mesh. My first simulations started out with a mesh sizing directly in between fine and coarse mesh size. After running the sim gathered data about the stress scene that showed the variation of stress across the whole column. This scene showed that the max stress was located at the bottom corner of the column at around a value of 6x10^6 N/m^2. The next scene I look at was deflection across the column at around 0.14 mm. The max deflection was at the top corner where the compressive force is being applied also at the same corner where the max stress was. The next scene that was created was a safety factor scene that allowed me to see how close our design material and vertical column was close to the limit of the safety factor for this design.
