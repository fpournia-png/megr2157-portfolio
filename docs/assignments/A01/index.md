## Decide
1. Homepage identity:
The intended audience for this portfolio is an employer or another student interested in what students have worked on in previous years. As a result the homepage should serve as an introduction to what the content on the portfolio is. An employer will have no idea what projects a student has worked on or what the portfolio may contain so it's important to give a detailed introduction that explains the content and purpose of the portfolio. If another student is reading the portfolio it’s important to highlight what has been worked on throughout the semester because year after year assignments may change. The homepage also identifies the three pillars that students go off of throughout the website, highlighting the core basis of all of the content on the portfolio. 


2. Intentional Customization: I changed the color of the header to purple from neon green. Personally, the neon green is harsh and not a color that is representative of something that I would want in my personal portfolio. Purple is also a color blind friendly color which allows the website to be more accessible to users who may be color blind. 


3. Your documentation standard: I intend to complete all assignments to the best of my ability, including thorough descriptions for all my projects and detailed reasoning behind my design decisions backed with theory and equations.
