#About Me
My name is Farima Pournia and I am currently studying Mechanical Engineering. My interest in engineering came from a passion for problem solving, an interest in understanding how systems work, and an interest in design. My passion for the automotive field is what led me to discover mechanical engineering. Mechanical engineering is one of the broadest disciplines of engineering which is why it is so applicable in all parts of the automotive field. As a mechanical engineer I study topics in other disciplines like computer, electrical, and civil engineering. Learning about topics from other disciplines allows me to have well rounded knowledge about all systems working together in a car, such as the electronics, and the chassis. My experience on my university's FSAE team has allowed me the unique opportunity to learn so much of the theory behind motorsports and also get hands-on experience that allows me to apply the knowledge I learn in the classroom to an actual engineering design project. On this team I work through an 18 month design cycle of designing, manufacturing and validating the aerodynamics components of the vehicle. Being on this team allows me to take a topic that I am interested and passionate about and actually go through the entire engineering design process similar to what I might do in industry. From design to simulation, to validation, and on track testing this simulates real world engineering. Throughout my time on the team I have learned many valuable skills that will help me throughout my engineering career. Some of these skills are technical such as how to validate designs before they are manufactured, how to analyze on track data to compare to simulated data, and how to tie fundamentals into design. Some of the soft skills I have learned include learning how to work well in a team, how to create timelines for a large project, or how to deal with team conflict. All of these skills have better prepared me for working in the engineering field. Throughout my time as an engineering student I hope to fully understand topics I learn by understanding the why behind theories in order to better understand how systems work in the world and how as an engineer I can solve problems.
